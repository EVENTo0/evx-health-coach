# EVX Health Coach — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 9, 2026

## Overview
EVX Health Coach ("EVX", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal health data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

## Data We Collect

### Health & Fitness Data
- Steps, heart rate, sleep, calories, weight (via HealthKit / Google Health Connect)
- Workout history and performance metrics
- Nutrition logs and meal plans

### Personal Information
- Email address (for account creation)
- Name (optional, for personalization)
- Health goals and fitness level (entered during onboarding)

### Lab Reports
- PDF or image files you upload for AI analysis
- Stored encrypted in Supabase private storage
- Never shared with third parties

### Usage Data
- App feature usage (anonymous analytics)
- Crash reports (no personal data attached)

## How We Use Your Data
1. Personalize AI-generated workouts, nutrition plans, and daily schedules
2. Analyze lab reports to provide educational health insights
3. Track your progress over time
4. Send push notifications (only if you opt in)

## Data Storage & Security
- All data stored in Supabase (SOC 2 Type II certified)
- Row-level security: you can only access your own data
- Health data never leaves your device unless explicitly uploaded
- OpenAI processes AI requests — no personal identifiers are sent

## Data Sharing
We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:
- Supabase (database hosting)
- OpenAI (AI processing — anonymized requests only)
- Expo (push notification delivery)

## Your Rights
- Export your data at any time from Settings
- Delete your account and all data from Settings > Delete Account
- Opt out of analytics in Settings > Privacy

## HealthKit / Google Health Connect
EVX requests read/write access to Apple HealthKit and Google Health Connect. This data is used solely to personalize your coaching experience. We never share health data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Children's Privacy
EVX is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
