## Full Description (4000 chars max)

EVX is your personal AI health coach — built for people who want results, not guesswork.

Whether you're training for performance, optimising your nutrition, or trying to understand your blood work, EVX gives you a premium coaching experience powered by AI.

**PERSONALISED WORKOUTS**
Tell EVX your goals, fitness level, and schedule. Get a custom workout plan with exercises, sets, reps, rest times, and modifications — generated in seconds and updated as you improve.

**INTELLIGENT NUTRITION**
Describe your dietary preferences, health goals, and any symptoms. EVX builds a complete meal plan with portions, hydration targets, and smart substitutions — no calorie counting required.

**LAB REPORT ANALYSIS**
Upload your blood work, hormone panels, or any lab results. EVX reads your biomarkers and provides clear, educational summaries — highlighting trends, lifestyle considerations, and what to discuss with your doctor.

**DAILY PLANNING**
EVX synthesises your workouts, meals, recovery status, and schedule into a clear daily action plan — so you always know exactly what to focus on.

**PROGRESS TRACKING**
Log your metrics over time. Visualise trends in strength, body composition, energy, and recovery. See your streaks grow and earn XP as you build consistency.

**EDUCATION LIBRARY**
Curated articles and videos across nutrition, training, recovery, mindset, and lab science — all written with evidence-based depth, not influencer fluff.

**BUILT FOR PRIVACY**
Your health data is yours. Encrypted storage, row-level security, and zero data selling — ever.
